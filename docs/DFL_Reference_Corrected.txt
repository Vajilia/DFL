FOREWORD: THE STORY OF THE SYSTEM
In 1988, you built an ant farm on graph paper. You moved the ants with dice and imagination. You were the only one who could see them.

Thirty-eight years later, the ants move themselves.

The Diamond Football League is not a game you play. It is a digital terrarium you observe. Forty-eight football teams exist in a pressure chamber where failure means erasure—eight teams each year vanish into Exile, banished from competition, forced to play meaningless games in empty stadiums while their fans vote on whether to fire their owners.

The survivors play an 18-game season on a tier-based schedule where your strength determines your opponents. The 1s battle the 1s. The 5s fight to avoid the bottom. Finish fifth, and you cease to exist for a year.

The Character Cards are the ants. Owners, GMs, Coaches, Players, Media, Fanbases—each has desires and fears. The Owner wants legacy and fears recall. The Player wants contracts and fears exile. The Media wants clicks and fears irrelevance. When two cards collide in an Interaction, they act in their own interests, not yours. They argue, scheme, betray, and occasionally achieve glory.

You do not roll dice. You watch. The Accounting Agent logs every choice. The Archive remembers every sin and triumph. The Dispatcher assigns four lightweight agents to play out scenes over two weeks of real time. You check in morning and evening. You see what the ants did while you were away.

This is not fantasy football. This is automated sports fiction—a league that writes its own novels, season by season, exile by exile, recall by recall.

The glass walls are built. The ants are loaded. The physics are set.

Welcome to the DFL.

THE EXILE SYSTEM
Trigger: Finish 5th in division
Duration: One season
Exiled Team Rights: Draft lottery, free agency (restricted), cap relief (50% absorption)
Exiled Team Games: Ambassador Season (7-game round-robin + Ambassador Bowl)
Return: As Tier 5 next season (easier schedule)
Lottery: 8-team weighted, 36 balls, worst record to best record: 8 / 7 / 6 / 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 balls (the best record of the eight holds 1 ball); four balls are drawn for picks 1-4, the other four teams pick 5-8 by record
THE SCHEDULE
Tiers: Each active team is assigned a Tier (1-5) in its division. (Playoff "seeds" are separate and run 1-7.)
Weeks	Matchup Type	Details
1-4	Non-Conference	1s vs 1s, 2s vs 2s, etc.
5-14	Conference	4 division games + 6 tier-based non-division
15-19	Divisional	4 games over 5 weeks (each team has one bye)
Tier-Based Non-Division:

1s play: 1s and 2s
2s play: 1s and 3s
3s play: 2s and 4s
4s play: 3s and 5s
5s play: 4s and 5s
THE PLAYOFFS
Field: Per conference, 4 division winners + 3 wild cards (7 seeds)
Wild Card: Seeds 2-7 play (3 games), Seed 1 bye
Divisional: 3 winners + Seed 1, re-seeded
Championship: 1 game per conference
Diamond Coronation (the final; the champion is presented with the Diamond Tiara): Last Sunday of calendar year
THE DRAFT
Picks	Teams
1-8	Exiled teams (lottery)
9-34	Active non-playoff teams (by record)
35-48	Playoff teams (by round, then record)
OWNER ACCOUNTABILITY
Fan Recall:

Rotation: 1 division per year, league-wide (8-year cycle: every owner is voted on once every 8 years)
Trigger: Owner approval <40% or team exiled
Vote: 1,000,000 fans, simple majority
Replacement: 5 candidates generated by Character Card Generator
Owner-Forced Sale:

Trigger: 2+ quarters of league subsidy
Vote: 25 of 48 owners
Result: Immediate termination, no appeals
Rule: No owner may own a DFL team twice. Ever.
