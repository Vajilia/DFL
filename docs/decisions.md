# DFL decision log

The authority for every rule is the living doc **"DFL Rulebook: The Synthesis"** (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0e15335e-d40e-4e7a-8d2a-e9eccedb24ea), frozen as v1 on 2026-10-06. It decides each rule by one test, in order: the Commissioner's skeleton, then the NFL rule, then the NFL rule adapted to 48 teams / 18 games / exile, then the fair-competitiveness bands. Its machine-readable form is `engine/rulebook.py`; `engine/check_rulebook.py` fails when the code and a rulebook row disagree, and writes `reports/rulebook_status.md` (every rule, its basis, and whether the code matches).

There is no "assumed" tag any more. Every rule has one of four bases (skeleton, nfl, adapted, fair); `engine/rules.py` carries the same labels, and simulation stand-ins that are not league rules are labelled `model`. The old decisions are archived in `docs/decisions_pre_rebase.md` (reference only).

The Commissioner delegated the open rulebook decisions to the AI on 2026-10-06 ("you have the authority"), to be decided by the NFL rule first and then by fair competitiveness. The alternatives for each are listed at the end of the Rulebook, so any row can be reopened.

To change a rule: change the number in `engine/rules.py`, update its row in `engine/rulebook.py`, and run `engine/check_rulebook.py`.

## Step 6a: earned-service foundation (2026-10-07)

Current decision order, authorized by the Commissioner: first ask what the NFL would likely do and why, adapted to DFL's schedule and structure; then test fair competitiveness; refer unresolved design choices to the Commissioner-elect. Existing DFL skeleton rules remain fixed.

The NFL distinguishes accrued seasons (six regular-season games on qualifying full-pay status, for free agency) from credited seasons (three, for minimum pay). Source: https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-free-agency/types-of-free-agents (checked 2026-10-07). Keeping the separate clocks prevents unsigned or practice-squad time from manufacturing free-agent rights or salary service.

`service.py` records qualifying games for the 53-player active/inactive roster and injured reserve, awards service once per season, and classifies expiring contracts as ERFA/RFA/UFA. Ambassador round-robin games count under the existing DFL rulebook. Byes, the Ambassador Bowl and playoffs do not count toward this regular-season service model. Practice-squad players earn service only after permanent promotion to the roster. Mid-season releases retain service earned before release; it is cumulative across teams, with a week counted only once. Counters and partial-year weeks persist through the existing player-body JSON save.

Historical limitation: founding roster players and old saves have no game-by-game service history, so their prior calendar-years estimate is retained once. Founding practice-squad players, drafted rookies and undrafted rookies start at zero. Future seasons are earned. NFI/PUP, suspensions, holdouts and injury settlements are not yet modelled and are not silently counted.

This is deliberately an accounting checkpoint: minimum salaries and practice-squad eligibility still use the old calendar-years field, and the re-signing policy is unchanged. The classifier does not enforce retention rights. Tenders, offer sheets, matching rights, tags, waivers and trade/pick ownership must be integrated before Step 6 can be called complete. Thus `fa.classes` has matching constants but a **partial** mechanism. The NFL itself makes ERFA rights conditional on offering a minimum one-year tender; classification alone cannot restrict a player.
