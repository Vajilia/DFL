# DFL glossary

This glossary distinguishes established terms from **proposed workflow terminology**. Where details are disputed, the approved rulebook and owner decisions take precedence.

- **DFL:** Diamond Football League, fictional autonomous American football simulation.
- **Franchise:** One of 48 persistent teams, with stable identity beyond a nickname or uniform change.
- **Active team:** One of 40 clubs competing in the regular season in a given year.
- **Exile:** The other eight clubs, one per division, in a recovery-oriented competition/state; not franchise deletion.
- **Tier:** Division finish-based schedule slot 1–5 for an active team; different from playoff seed.
- **Schedule skeleton:** Opponent/time slot structure that maps to teams by current tier placement. Do not equate a nickname with a scheduling ID.
- **Diamond Coronation:** Championship final; winner earns the Diamond Tiara.
- **Ambassador Season:** Competition for the exiled clubs.
- **CEO:** Franchise executive accountable to its fanbase; has oversight of football leadership but not unrestricted GM/HC operational authority.
- **GM:** General manager responsible for acquiring personnel and assembling roster resources.
- **HC:** Head coach responsible for coaching, deployment, and final team readiness; exact cut/authority boundaries require code audit.
- **Coordinator:** Coach with a delegated scheme/play-calling sphere, subject to HC oversight; the owner has requested three coordinator positions, implementation to be verified.
- **Commissioner (in-world):** League governance office, distinct from the human project owner.
- **Commissioner (human):** Historical documentation sometimes calls the real-world project owner 'Commissioner'; never conflate this with an AI's authority over production.
- **Character card:** Persistent history, traits, perceptions, and goals used for bounded decisions.
- **Decision Point:** Engine-supplied actor, perceived context, and legal options; driver chooses an action, not its outcome.
- **Autopilot:** Deterministic/default choice policy when no external agent decides.
- **Truth (proposed engineering term):** Approved specification plus separately tracked stable release; do not treat code and specification as identical.
- **Beta (proposed):** Isolated experimentation.
- **Alpha (proposed):** Acceptance candidate awaiting owner approval.
- **Fair-competitiveness bands:** Engine constraints intended to bound league outcomes; verify exact definitions in source.
- **Source of truth:** A designated authoritative specification or dataset, not necessarily the newest file by timestamp.
