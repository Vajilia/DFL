# Payroll and the cap

4 leagues x 48 seasons, fast engine, autopilot, first 8 seasons dropped. Every number is the AI's placeholder pay scale (economy.py); the rules ($100M cap that never inflates, $125M most a team may go into a season with, 90% floor over four seasons, exiled teams' payroll counted at half) are the Commissioner's.

## Where payrolls sit

Mean payroll 98.8M of the $100M cap; the middle 90% of team-seasons run 85.8M to 112.2M. 10.8% of team-seasons end below the 90% floor and 6.7% end at their limit. The five best-paid players take 28% of a team's payroll on average.

## Banked room

A team carries on average 11.6M of banked room into a season (most possible 25M); 31% of team-seasons begin with the full 25M and 28% with none.

## Dead money

A team carries on average 10.3M of dead money (bonus and guaranteed pay of players it let go); 6.0 cuts a season use one of the two post-draft designations that split the hit across two seasons.

## The Equalization Fund

Each season the Fund takes in 340M of forfeited room and pays out the league's half of exiled teams' payroll, 392M. In a year it falls short the 48 teams cover it equally (an average levy of 48M a season); above its 100M reserve the surplus goes equally to the teams that played (an average of 3M a season). Floor top-ups paid to players average 3.1M a season across the league.

## What the cap costs teams

Each season the league re-signs 1155 expiring players, loses 387.2 it could not afford to keep, and teams that spent banked room cut 23.4 contracts to get back under the cap.
