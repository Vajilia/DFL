# Payroll and the cap

4 leagues x 48 seasons, fast engine, autopilot, first 8 seasons dropped. Every number is the AI's placeholder pay scale (economy.py); the rules ($100M cap that never inflates, $125M most a team may go into a season with, 90% floor, exiled teams' payroll counted at half) are Jeph's.

## Where payrolls sit

Mean payroll 94.7M of the $100M cap; the middle 90% of team-seasons run 82.0M to 107.4M. 28.2% of team-seasons end below the 90% floor and 8.2% end at their limit. The five best-paid players take 34% of a team's payroll on average.

## Banked room

A team carries on average 20.5M of banked room into a season (most possible 25M); 65% of team-seasons begin with the full 25M and 7% with none.

## The pool

Each season the league takes in 552M of forfeited room and 50M of floor shortfalls and absorbs 375M of exiled teams' payroll. The pool balance at the end of a league averaged 7542M (range 6326M to 8459M). A positive balance means forfeits and shortfalls have outrun the relief the league absorbs, so the pool has surplus nobody spends yet (what to do with it is open).

## What the cap costs teams

Each season the league re-signs 417 expiring players, loses 3.3 it could not afford to keep, and teams that spent banked room cut 0.1 contracts to get back under the cap.
