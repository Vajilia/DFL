# Club finance

3 leagues x 40 seasons, fast engine, autopilot, first 8 seasons dropped. Every dollar level is a model dial (finance.py); the draw cap ($100M), the 10 to 20 year tenure, the 34% ticket pool and the forced-sale vote are the Commissioner's rules.

## Revenue and surplus

Mean revenue 195M a club; mean surplus 60M (lowest -17M, 10th percentile 10M, 90th 96M, highest 170M). 2.9% of club-seasons end in a loss.

## The CEO's draw and the Fund

Mean draw 48M. 1.3% of club-seasons hit the $100M cap (62 of 4608); the excess sent to the Equalization Fund averages 6.2M a season across the league.

## Subsidies and forced sales

Subsidies from CEOs' draws average 0.1M a season; CEOs put 0.0M a season of their own money into clubs the reserve and subsidies could not cover. 1 forced-sale votes were called in 96 league-seasons; 1 owners were forced to sell.

## Tenure

155 CEOs completed their tenure: average 13.9 seasons (shortest 10, longest 20). Recalls and forced sales end tenures early and are not counted here.
